# Installation

If you're reading this, then you've successfully installed the pairs stratification utility already.

Installation is a simple matter of running the installer executable.

Installing over the top of an existing version is harmless - but see the note about your Options below.

## Uninstalling

You can uninstall through your operating system's Settings or Control Panel.

Uninstalling does not remove any of your data files, including outputs generated by the utility, but **will remove your Options settings**.

## Retaining your Options

To keep your Options, copy the file "config.json" somewhere safe BEFORE uninstalling and reinstalling. This file is located in the installation folder (default C:\PairsStrat).

Upgrading to a newer version without uninstalling first will keep your options settings.
